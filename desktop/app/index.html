    How Transit Overlay works

    1. Get your network in.With the mod: click its button at the top left of the game. Without it: ⚙ Settings → Game connection → copy the command, run it in the game console, then import the log.
    2. Pick a tab.Lines draws and plans lines. Coverage shows what your stops reach and where a new one pays off. Cargo shows industries, shops and what they are missing. Schematic makes a metro-style map.
    3. Use the tools.Inspect: click anything to see what it is and who uses it. Station: add a planned stop. Draw: draw a line. Edit: move points, add bends, cut. Hover a stop for its card in any tool.
